Terms & conditions

The terms on which we sell, deliver and guarantee what we make.

Who you are contracting with

These terms apply to purchases from Naqsh Carpets, trading from Ajman, United Arab Emirates. By placing an order you accept them.

Prices and payment

All prices are in UAE dirhams and include VAT at the prevailing rate. Prices shown on this website apply to UAE delivery; shipping outside the UAE is quoted separately before you confirm.

Stocked items are paid in full at the point of order. Custom and made-to-order work requires a 30% deposit to confirm, with the balance due on completion before dispatch.

Orders and availability

An order is accepted when we confirm it, not when it is placed. If an item is unavailable or a price has been published in error we will tell you promptly and refund in full. We are not obliged to supply at an incorrectly published price.

Delivery

Delivery dates are estimates given in good faith. We are not liable for delays caused by customs, weather, freight carriers or events outside our control. Risk passes to you on delivery.

Returns

Stocked items may be returned within fourteen days as set out in our returns policy. Custom and made-to-order pieces are excluded, because they are manufactured to your specification.

Variation in handmade goods

Hand-knotted, hand-tufted and hand-dyed rugs vary. Colour, dimensions and pattern may differ slightly from photographs and between pieces, and abrash — natural horizontal colour variation from different dye lots — is a characteristic of hand-dyed wool, not a defect. Dimensions are subject to a tolerance of ±3%.

Guarantee

We guarantee against manufacturing defect: for the lifetime of the rug on hand-knotted pieces, and three years on hand-tufted and machine-made. The guarantee does not cover normal wear, fading from direct sunlight, damage from incorrect cleaning, moth damage, or shedding in the first weeks of use, which is normal.

Liability

Our liability for any order is limited to the amount you paid for it. Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates and the Emirate of Ajman, and the courts of Ajman have exclusive jurisdiction.
