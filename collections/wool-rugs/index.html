Wool Rugs

Wool is still the best rug fibre there is, for reasons that are chemical rather than sentimental. Each fibre is coated in lanolin, which repels liquid long enough for you to blot a spill. It is naturally flame-retardant. It is elastic, so the pile recovers from compression. And it holds dye deep rather than on the surface.

Buying wool rugs: what to look at

We hold 96 designs in this collection, from AED 1,219 to AED 6,124 depending on size. Everything here is made to order in four to six weeks.

Constructions in this collection: hand-tufted. Principal fibres: wool. Available sizes run 120×170 cm, 80×300 cm, 160×230 cm, 200×200 cm, 200×300 cm, 250×350 cm, and any piece can be woven to a custom dimension.

How to choose between them

  • Start with the room, not the rug. Measure the seating group or the table, not the floor. Our rug size guide has the numbers for every room.
  • Then choose construction for how hard the room works. A hallway or dining room wants a flatweave or a low pile; a majlis or bedroom can take depth.
  • Then choose the design. Pattern hides wear and suits a busy family room; plain and tonal suits a room that already has a lot going on.

Delivery, trial and returns

Delivery is free anywhere in the UAE and usually next working day from stock. Every rug comes with a 14-day home trial — live with it, and if it is wrong we collect it and refund in full. For the GCC we ship duties-prepaid in five to eight working days, and we ship worldwide to the USA and Europe.

Not sure which one?Send a photo of the room and one measurement on WhatsApp. We will shortlist three pieces from this collection and tell you honestly which is the better buy for the room — including when the cheaper one is the right answer.
