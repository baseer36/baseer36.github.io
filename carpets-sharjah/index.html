Rugs & Carpets in Sharjah

Free delivery, home trial and free measuring across Sharjah — from our showroom in Ajman.

Rugs and carpets delivered across Sharjah

We are an Ajman showroom, and Sharjah is about twenty minutes from our Ajman showroom. Delivery to Sharjah is free on every order, and we deliver and lay the rug ourselves rather than handing it to a courier — which matters for anything over 200 × 300 cm, because a large rug arrives rolled and needs laying flat to settle.

Areas we deliver to regularly: Al Majaz, Al Khan, Al Nahda, Muwaileh, Al Qasimia, Al Taawun, University City — and everywhere else in Sharjah.

What we bring to you

  • Samples at home. We bring physical samples so you can see the wool and the colour in your own light, which is the only place the decision is actually made.
  • A 14-day home trial. We lay the rug, you live with it for two weeks, and if it is wrong we collect it and refund in full.
  • Measuring for custom work. Free survey anywhere in Sharjah for majlis carpets, wall-to-wall installations and shaped pieces.
  • Collection for cleaning. We collect rugs for specialist hand-washing and return them clean, dry and wrapped.

Majlis and mosque carpet in Sharjah

A large share of our Sharjah work is majlis carpet and masjid installations. Both need a site survey — a majlis because the room is rarely a true rectangle, and a prayer hall because the rows have to be set square to the qibla wall. We survey, plan the cuts and install, and the survey is free.
